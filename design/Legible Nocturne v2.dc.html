02 · The mission

What Legible is, and why the trip is worth taking.

Legible is an accessibility reader for the web. Point it at a site and it walks every page the way a screen-reader user does — in order, out loud, keyboard only — then hands back a plain-language report: what broke, who it stops, and the line of markup that caused it.

01

Catch what a score cannot

Automated checkers grade colour and markup. Legible grades the experience: unlabelled controls, focus that vanishes, headings that lead nowhere.

02

Ship fixes, not findings

Every failure arrives with its element, the rule it breaks and a suggested patch — ranked by how many people it unblocks first.

03

Stay audit-ready

Re-read on every deploy and keep a dated AA record for all twenty-eight pages, instead of a screenshot from last quarter.
